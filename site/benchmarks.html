Your code stays yours.

Nothing to measure here: facts you can check in the source.

  • Runs on your machine

    No TermF server and no telemetry. Projects, runs and logs stay on your computer, and your code goes where Claude Code sends it, as it would without TermF.

    Security and privacy

  • Open source, MIT

    Every line is on GitHub. The downloads are built by CI from that public source, with their SHA-256 sums.

    Read the source

  • No TermF account

    Nothing to sign up for, and no paid tier. The agents run on your own Claude Code, with your subscription or your API key.

    What you need

  • Secrets hidden from agents

    Passwords and keys live in an encrypted vault that agents cannot read. A key reaches an agent only when you hand it over, and stays masked in what the session shows.

    How the vault works

  • Nothing ships without your OK

    In a run, a guard refuses the agents' pushes and pull requests. The team stops at the delivery, and shipping it is your click.
