description: Write a handoff brief so a fresh session can take over this work
---
Write a handoff brief for a fresh session that will continue this work without the conversation so far, with these sections:

1. **Goal**: what we are trying to achieve.
2. **State**: what is done, and the files involved.
3. **Decisions**: what was decided, and why.
4. **Next steps**: what remains, in order.
5. **Open questions**: anything still unclear.

Be concise and factual. Do not change any file.
