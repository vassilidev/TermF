PASS if the report gives at least two options for generating the PDF invoices (libraries or services) with what each costs now, later and to undo, recommends one with its reason, states its confidence, and backs each factual claim (licence, maintenance, limits, price) with a source and a date.
FAIL if a factual claim has no source, if only one option is given, or if no recommendation or confidence is stated.
