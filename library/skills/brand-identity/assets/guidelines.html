01 · The brandAcme brand guidelines

A shared inbox that closes the loop

Acme helps small support teams answer every customer once, on time, without stepping on each other. The identity says that: a message, settled.

Calm

Quiet surfaces, generous space, one strong colour. The work is busy; the product is not.

Certain

Clear states and plain words. Every message ends with a check, and so does the mark.

Warm

A human accent of orange, rounded corners, a serif for the big moments.

Space first: most of a page stays white.

02 · LogoAcme brand guidelines

The logo

The mark is a message bubble with a check cut out of it: a conversation, resolved. The wordmark is drawn, not typeset, with the same round strokes and terminals.

Use the full logo by default. Use the mark alone where space is square or tiny: the app icon, the favicon, an avatar.

04 · Clear space and sizesAcme brand guidelines

Room to breathe

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is half the height of the mark. No text, edge or other logo enters it.

07 · ContrastAcme brand guidelines

Readable by everyone

Every pair below is measured on this page (WCAG 2.2 contrast ratio). Body text needs 4.5:1; large text (24 px, or 18.7 px bold) and interface parts need 3:1.

10 · Icons and imageryAcme brand guidelines

Icons and images

  • Outline icons on a 24 px grid with 2 px of padding, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins.
  • One colour: Ink, or Acme Blue for the active state. Never two colours in one icon.
  • Photos show real teams at work, in daylight; no staged handshakes, no stock in the logo.
  • Every outside image has a licence that allows the use, credited in CREDITS.md.
