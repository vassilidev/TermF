---
name: conventional-commits
description: Commit messages follow Conventional Commits
---
- Write commit messages as Conventional Commits: `type(scope): summary`, the type being one of feat, fix, docs, style, refactor, perf, test, build, ci, chore, revert.
- The summary is in the imperative mood, lower case, without a final period, at most 72 characters.
- Mark a breaking change with `!` after the type or scope and a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.
